# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small software teams use Pixel Office as a shared virtual room for lightweight
presence, movement, status updates, and conversation during the workday.

## Product Purpose

Pixel Office makes remote teamwork feel spatial and playful without becoming a
large virtual world. Success means a four-person team can quickly understand
who is present, move around one believable office, and chat without friction.

## Brand Personality

Warm, playful, and focused. The product should feel like a small creative
software studio that has been actively used, not a formal workplace simulator.

## Anti-references

- Corporate floor plans with strict symmetry and empty corridors
- Oversized virtual worlds that make small teams feel lost
- Decorative game UI that obscures status, chat, or movement
- Flat furniture layouts that read as a spreadsheet grid

## Design Principles

1. Keep one room rich enough to feel inhabited.
2. Preserve readable workflows beneath the playful presentation.
3. Use asymmetry and compact clusters to create a lived-in studio.
4. Keep movement paths obvious without drawing corridor lines.
5. Let character presence and conversation remain the visual focus.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for interface text and controls. Preserve keyboard
operation, clear focus states, reduced-motion behavior, and information that
does not depend on color alone.
